Verifying this report independently

Every claim above is checkable on any Bitcoin node with the chain, without a wallet or an index:

  1. Each proofs bundle: bip322-audit verify PROOFS.json checks the signatures against the addresses, the stamp block, and that every listed output existed at the stamp with the stated amount (and, for outputs spent since, that they were unspent at the stamp).
  2. Each output in the balances: bitcoin-cli gettxout TXID VOUT for unspent ones, or the creating transaction by its block hash.
  3. The movements: each transaction by txid, its inputs and outputs as listed.
