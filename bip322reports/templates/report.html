6. Basis of preparation and limitations

  • Point in time. Holdings are stated as of the blocks named; nothing here says what the wallet holds after block {{ r.closing.height }}.
  • Control, not title. A proof shows that the keys behind an address signed the message. It does not show who owns the coins, whether they are pledged, lent or otherwise encumbered, or what liabilities stand against them.
  • Completeness. The movements and holdings are those of this wallet as known to the holder's node. That the wallet is the holder's only one, or that all of its addresses are in the node, rests on the holder's representation; no cryptographic check establishes it.
  • Proofs are per address. An address proven once is covered for every output paid to it. The date of each proof is its stamp block; the checks above say whether it postdates the period.
  • No wallet structure is disclosed. The statement and the bundles name addresses and outputs only; they carry no descriptor, extended public key or derivation path.

7. Verification procedures

Every figure and proof above is checkable on any Bitcoin node with the chain, without a wallet or an index. The bundles are in ledger/ next to this statement.

  1. Proofs. For each bundle: bip322-audit verify ledger/<bundle>/proofs.json. This checks every signature against its address, the stamp block, and that each listed output existed at the stamp with the stated amount (and, for outputs spent since, that they were unspent at the stamp). For a second opinion on a signature, bip322-refcheck ADDRESS SIGNATURE MESSAGE runs independent implementations beside it.
  2. Closing holdings. For each output in section 3: bitcoin-cli gettxout TXID VOUT shows it unspent with the stated amount and address, or, if spent since, its creating transaction by txid and block shows the same.
  3. Opening holdings and movements. Each transaction in section 2 by txid, with the inputs and outputs listed; the running balance follows from the opening balance and the net of each.
  4. Reference. The reference {{ r.report_id }} in the footer is derived from the figures stated; report.json carries the same reference and every figure in machine-readable form.
